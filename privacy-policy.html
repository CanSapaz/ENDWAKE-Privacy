ENDWAKE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ENDWAKE is an offline, text-based survival game published by Fidovasoft. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: the game runs entirely on your device. We do not run servers, there are no accounts, and we cannot see anything you do in the game.

Data we collect

None. The developer of ENDWAKE collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry, no advertising identifiers and no location data. There is no sign-up and no user account.

Data the game keeps on your device

The name you enter at the start, your current story, saved games, your archive of past lives and endings, discoveries and settings are stored locally on your device only, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.

  • Sharing a story card. When you choose to share a story report, the game draws an image on your device and hands it to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is decided by the app you pick. If you choose "Save Image", the system asks for permission to add it to your photo library. ENDWAKE never reads your photos.
  • Contact. "Contact" in Settings opens your own mail app with our address. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
  • Deleting data. Settings > Delete all progress removes everything the game has stored. Uninstalling the app does the same. Because there is no cloud copy, we cannot recover it for you.

Purchases

The current version has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a future version, they will be processed entirely by the store you installed the game from (Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone and iPad), the game will never see your payment details, and this page will be updated before that version is released.

Network access

ENDWAKE works fully offline. The game makes no network requests of its own.

Third parties

There are no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporters or social login providers in ENDWAKE, and the game does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

ENDWAKE is a dark survival story for teens and adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Any feature that would send data off your device would be opt-in.
