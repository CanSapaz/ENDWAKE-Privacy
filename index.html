ENDWAKE

Every decision leads to a different world.

ENDWAKE is an offline, text-based survival game. Survive a zombie outbreak or a continent-wide blackout, decide who to trust, and see the chain of choices behind every ending. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: fidovasoft@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the game version from Settings.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. There is no cloud copy, so deleting the game deletes your progress.
  • How do I start over? Settings > Delete current story ends the ongoing life and keeps your archive. Settings > Delete all progress removes everything.
  • Can animal companions die? Yes, unless you turn on Settings > Spare the animals.
  • Does it need the internet? No. Everything runs on the device. Only buying a world or restoring purchases goes through the store.
  • I bought a world on another device. How do I get it back? Sign in with the same Google Play or Apple account, then Store > Restore purchases (also in Settings > Purchases).
